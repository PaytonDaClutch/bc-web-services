Questions

Answers taken from pages they already published.

Their FAQ page currently mixes one real roofing question with leftover Wix placeholder questions. Only the published roofing guidance, hours, and service area are used here.

When do I need a new roof?

From their FAQ: it can be difficult to know when a roof needs to be replaced. They are willing to inspect the roof and give an estimated time frame. They write that most common roofs have about a 30-year lifespan to start with, depending on the quality of materials, the quality of the installation, and the maintenance that has been done. Things to look out for: obvious leaks, missing granules on composition that show fraying or bare spots, and any holes or broken shingles.
