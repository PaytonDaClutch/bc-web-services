How we coach

Coached, not crowded.

Every class follows the same three rules.

  1. 01

    A coach on every set

    Every lift is watched and cued. No screens, no guesswork.

  2. 02

    Eight to a class

    Small groups mean you get real feedback, not a nod from across the room.

  3. 03

    A plan, not a workout

    Training runs in six-week blocks, so you can see the numbers move.

Questions

Before your first class

I've never lifted a barbell. Can I join?

Yes. Start with the free intro session. We teach every lift from an empty bar.

What should I bring?

Flat shoes, water, and clothes you can squat in. Chalk and belts are on the floor.

How long is a class?

Barbell classes run 60 minutes. Conditioning runs 45.

Can I just drop in?

Yes, for $32 a class (sample price). Book ahead, since classes cap at eight.

Is there parking?

Street parking out front and a small lot behind the building.
