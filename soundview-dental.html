01 / First visit

What your first visit includes, minute by minute.

No surprises. Here is exactly what happens in a new-patient appointment, so you know what you are walking into.

  1. 0–10 min

    A real conversation first

    Your dentist asks what is bothering you, what has gone badly before, and what you want out of the visit. Nothing starts until you are ready.

  2. 10–25 min

    Low-dose digital X-rays

    Only the images you need, shown on the screen beside you so you can see what we see.

  3. 25–60 min

    Exam and gentle cleaning

    A full exam, gum check and oral cancer screening, then a cleaning paced by you. Raise a hand any time to pause.

  4. 60–75 min

    A written plan, in plain English

    Anything we find is explained with photos and listed by priority, with a cost estimate before any treatment is booked.

03 / Comfort

Nervous about the dentist? Plenty of our patients start there.

Ask for any of these when you book, or on the day. You never have to explain why.

AAgreed stop signal

Raise your left hand and everything stops. We agree on it before we start.

BTell, show, then do

Each step is described and shown before it happens. No surprise sounds.

CHeadphones & blankets

Noise-cancelling headphones, a weighted blanket and a ceiling screen.

DNumbing that waits

Topical gel first, and we check it is working before anything else.

EQuiet first slot

Book the 7 am chair: no waiting room, no rush, no crowd.

FSedation options

Nitrous oxide is available after a health review. Ask your dentist.

You are in charge of the pace. If you had a hard experience somewhere else, say so on the booking form. Your dentist will read it before you arrive.
