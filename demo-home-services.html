What we fix

Small jobs, done properly.

Every service follows the same deal: we name the problem, quote the fix before starting, and stand behind the result.

Drywall & paint

The problem: dents, doorknob holes, water stains that never got fixed. The fix: patch, texture-match, prime and paint so it disappears. The proof: you can’t find the patch in daylight.

Doors & carpentry

Questions

Asked often.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. Small jobs are quoted flat and itemised before we start. The only exception is the half-day block, which is a flat four hours against your list.

How fast can you come out?

Most jobs are booked within three days. Genuine emergencies — active leaks, a door that won’t close — get the first slot we can move.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Workmanship is warranted for 12 months. If our fix fails, we come back and make it right at no charge.

Do you supply parts and materials?

Either way. We’ll supply quality parts at cost with receipts, or fit what you’ve already bought — the quote says which, before we start.
